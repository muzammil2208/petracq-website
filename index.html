FAQ

Quarry & mining management system: common questions

What is a quarry and mining management system?

A quarry and mining management system is software that runs the day-to-day operation of a quarry, mine or stone crusher plant. It records every vehicle and weighment, manages loading and lab checks, generates GST and royalty invoices, controls customer credit and gives owners live reports across plants. Petracq does all of this in one system.

Does Petracq work with my existing weighbridge?

Yes. Petracq connects to the weighbridge indicator over RS232 and captures tare and gross weight automatically. The Windows weighbridge app keeps working when the internet drops and syncs once you are back online.

Can Petracq handle royalty and GST billing for quarries?

Yes. Petracq bills from the approved net weight, supports multiple GST slabs on one invoice, such as material and royalty, and manages royalty books with unique slip numbers and low-stock alerts.

Is Petracq suitable for multiple quarries or crusher plants?

Yes. Petracq is multi-plant ready, with plant-wise settings, plant-wise invoice numbering and users who see only their own plant's data, while owners see dashboards across every plant.

Which devices does Petracq run on?

Petracq runs on the web, Android, iOS, Windows and macOS. Loader and lab staff use mobile apps, the weighbridge runs on a Windows desktop app, and customers use an online portal.

Who is Petracq built for?

Petracq is built for quarry and mining operators, stone crusher and aggregate plant owners, sand and GSB suppliers, and multi-plant groups in India.
