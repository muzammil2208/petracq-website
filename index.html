The problems we solve

Running a crusher plant leaks money in familiar ways.

Petracq closes each of these gaps.

See it on your workflow
  • Weights typed by hand, open to error and manipulation
    Tare and gross weight captured directly from the weighbridge
  • Dispatches over the credit limit, with no check at the gate
    Credit checked before dispatch, with over-limit vehicles blocked or sent for approval
  • Material or plant switched after the vehicle is already inside
    Changes need an approved request, with CCTV snapshots on key events
  • Royalty slips misused, duplicated or running out unnoticed
    Royalty books with unique numbers and low-stock alerts
  • Moisture disputes with no record to settle them
    Lab results recorded and deductions calculated by your material rules
  • Paperwork and phone calls between the weighbridge, loaders and accounts
    Loader and lab tasks sent straight to their phones
  • No clear picture across multiple plants
    Live dashboards and reports, filtered by plant, customer or material

What you get

Everything your plant runs on, in one place.

Nine modules that work together, from the gate to the customer's inbox.

01

Weighbridge integration, even offline

  • Direct RS232 connection with automatic tare and gross capture
  • Windows desktop app that keeps working when the internet drops
  • Automatic sync once you're back online, with no duplicates
  • Weighment slips printed or shared digitally
02

Complete vehicle lifecycle tracking

  • Every stage from entry to exit, from verification to billing
  • One active transaction per vehicle
  • Live status of every vehicle in the plant
  • Approval-based material and plant change requests
  • CCTV snapshots linked to every key event
03

Billing, GST & royalty built in

  • Automatic billing from approved net weight
  • Multiple GST slabs on one invoice, such as material and royalty
  • Royalty books with unique numbers, multiple slips per trip and low-stock alerts
  • Plant-wise invoice numbering and PDF invoices
  • Payment tracking, partial payments and advances
04

Credit control at the gate

Customer credit is checked before dispatch and billing. Over-limit vehicles are blocked or routed for approval, and every override is logged.

05

Moisture testing & weight comparison

  • Lab staff record moisture results from a mobile app
  • Deductions calculated automatically by your material-wise rules
  • Customers upload destination weights, and differences beyond your tolerance are flagged
06

Mobile apps for Android & iOS

  • Loader App: receive loading tasks, update status, work offline
  • Lab App: see pending tests, enter results, sync automatically
  • Real-time notifications, task history and device-level access control
07

Customer portal

  • Track dispatches in real time
  • Download invoices, weighment slips and royalty documents
  • View statements, balances and credit status
  • Upload their own weighment data

Fewer phone calls and faster payment reconciliation.

08

Dashboards, reports & analytics

  • Live admin, operator and customer dashboards
  • Operational, financial, plant, customer and machine reports
  • Filter by plant, date, customer, material or vehicle
  • Export to PDF and Excel, with scheduled email delivery
09

Control, security & compliance

  • Role-based access you configure without code changes
  • Maker-checker approvals, so nobody approves their own request
  • Tamper-proof audit logs of every critical action
  • Multi-plant support, with users seeing only their plant's data
  • Breakdown tracking, document management and notifications
  • Integration with CCTV, SMS/email and accounting software

How it works

One vehicle, gate to invoice.

Every trip follows the same controlled path. Each step is recorded, so nothing gets skipped and nothing goes unbilled.

  1. 1

    Vehicle arrives

    Operator verifies the vehicle, customer and credit.

  2. 2

    First weighment

    Tare weight is captured automatically.

  3. 3

    Loader assigned

    The task is sent to the loader's phone.

  4. 4

    Loading and lab check

    Moisture is tested where applicable.

  5. 5

    Final weighment

    Net weight is calculated.

  6. 6

    Invoice and slip

    GST and royalty are applied and documents generated.

  7. 7

    Customer notified

    Documents are available instantly in the portal.
